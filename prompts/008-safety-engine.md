# Cursor Prompt 008 — Safety Engine

Build a multi-stage safety system.

Stage 1:
Normalize text.

Stage 2:
Blocked-term and fuzzy matching.

Stage 3:
IP/trademark/brand/franchise risk classification.

Stage 4:
AI text review.

Stage 5:
Image review after generation.

Stage 6:
Final decision.

Decisions:
PASS
REVIEW
REJECT

Hard reject:
- recognizable copyrighted characters
- logos
- game assets
- sports team marks
- celebrity likenesses
- copied slogans
- hateful content
- slurs
- threats
- graphic violence
- explicit sexual content

Never claim legal clearance.

Log every decision and policy version.
